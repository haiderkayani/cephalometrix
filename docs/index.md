### Why CephalometriX?

CephalometriX simplifies cephalometric landmark detection through:

- 💡 Intuitive preprocessing and landmark manipulation
- 🔁 Built-in dataset loaders and scalable design
- 🧠 Ready for deep learning integration
- 📐 Ideal for research, prototyping, and clinical toolchains
